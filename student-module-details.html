About this Module

This module provides a rigorous foundation in the science of climate change. It explores the Earth's energy balance, the greenhouse effect, and the role of anthropogenic emissions in altering global climate systems. Designed for professionals seeking to ground their sustainability initiatives in sound science, the course covers historical climate data, future projections, and the complex feedback loops that accelerate warming.

Participants will engage with data from the IPCC and other leading scientific bodies, developing the ability to critically analyze climate reports and communicate scientific findings to non-technical stakeholders.

done_all Learning Objectives

  • check_circle Understand the fundamental physical science basis of the climate system.
  • check_circle Analyze human influence on the climate and quantify greenhouse gas emissions.
  • check_circle Evaluate projected global and regional climate changes under various scenarios.
  • check_circle Assess climate risks and the necessity of immediate mitigation actions.
